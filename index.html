Privacy Policy – Pinterest Pin Manager Last updated: 15 November 2026 1. Introduction Pinterest Pin Manager is a privately operated application designed for personal use. It allows the account owner to manage and publish content to their Pinterest account. 2. Data Collection The application accesses Pinterest account information, boards, and pins through the official Pinterest API. It processes images, titles, descriptions, and other content supplied by the account owner. 3. Use of Data Data is used exclusively to: Create and manage Pinterest pins. Retrieve available Pinterest boards. Generate suggested pin titles, descriptions, and tags. Publish content to the account owner's Pinterest account. 4. Data Sharing Personal data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising purposes. 5. Data Storage and Security Pinterest authentication credentials are stored securely and are used only to access the Pinterest API. Appropriate measures are taken to prevent unauthorised access. 6. Data Deletion The account owner can revoke the application's Pinterest access at any time through their Pinterest account settings. Locally stored application data can also be deleted by the account owner. 7. Contact For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the application owner through the contact details associated with the Pinterest account.
